Safezone [Web]

My role

System Architect & Core Developer— contributed to system architecture, database design, and implementation of core features for a disaster incident reporting and response platform.

A web platform designed for the Santa Maria, Bulacan MDRRMO to manage incident reports, coordinate emergency responders, and share operational updates. Features configurable incident reporting, geospatial barangay mapping, responder tracking, and post-operation reporting workflows.

Preview
  1. Live incident dashboard for reviewing, verifying, updating, and resolving emergency reports
  2. Interactive mapping with barangay boundaries, hazard geofences, evacuation centers, and incident clustering
  3. AI-assisted report and evidence analysis with final verification controlled by administrators
  4. Responder dispatch and operations tracking based on capability, availability, and proximity
  5. Runtime configuration of incident types, intake questions, geofences, responder requirements, and verification thresholds

002 Mobile

2026

Safezone [Mobile]

Live
My role

Sole Mobile Developer — independently designed and implemented the SafeZone mobile application for residents and emergency responders.

A cross-platform disaster response application built for the Santa Maria, Bulacan MDRRMO. Residents can submit incident reports, request emergency assistance, and receive announcements, while responders can participate in operations, share live situation updates, submit post-operation reports, and coordinate field activities.

Preview
  1. Guided emergency reporting with dynamic questions, GPS location, photo evidence, and confidential reporter details
  2. Offline-ready reports, assistance requests, and SOS submissions with automatic retry
  3. Live safety map with hazard alerts, evacuation-center capacity, and navigation routes
  4. Emergency assistance and SOS workflows for medical, rescue, evacuation, and fire-response needs
  5. Responder field operations with dispatch alerts, mission briefings, navigation, status updates, and post-operation reports
